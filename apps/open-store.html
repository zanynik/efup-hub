What would have to be true?

This is a model, not a forecast. The open store leans on two beliefs. Move them to where you actually think they are, and see whether the store still works.

Everything else is fixed and written down in the model: 400 households spending €220 a month, shelf prices 25% above cost, €9,000 of fixed costs, 3% lost to energy and spoilage, €16 an hour for work nobody volunteers for. A broken freezer in March, a shortage in June, a discount chain in September.
