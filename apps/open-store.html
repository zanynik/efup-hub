Where the numbers come from

    Everything else is an assumption written down in the model: a €170 monthly basket at wholesale cost, 1.3 hours of shop work per household, €16 an hour, rent and core staff of €3,400 to €3,900 a month, and a supermarket chain that buys 5% cheaper, is handier, and cuts prices when it loses shoppers. Applying lab and field findings to a grocery store is itself an assumption.
