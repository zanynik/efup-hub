Why? The fear of survival is the most core fear. If food approaches free, the grip loosens. The store isn't a charity — it's a conscious act of non-craving structurally embedded in a business. Any surplus is transparently refunded, embodying the practice of letting go.

Scale path: pop-up → store → chain → food company → supply chain. As prices spiral down, the gift economy emerges.
